# Learner-Facing Copy

Use this workflow before publishing copy that was written by AI or by an external reviewer. Source quotations, transcript excerpts, and instructor wording remain locked unless the learner explicitly asks for an editorial transformation.

## Two Passes

Review the whole artifact, not isolated sentences.

1. **Editorial pass:** mark each block `delete`, `retain`, or `shorten`. Remove repetition before rewriting anything.
2. **Writing pass:** rewrite only the retained content in the voice and reading level appropriate to the learner and subject.

Read the result as a continuous lesson. A locally polished sentence can still repeat the title, adjacent control, or previous paragraph.

## Keep Internal Work Internal

Do not transfer conversation residue into the lesson. Keep these in working notes unless the learner needs them to act or avoid a real error:

- rejected alternatives and implementation history;
- explanations of why the page was designed a certain way;
- arbitrary repetition limits or temporary experiments discussed in chat;
- instructions to the AI, reviewer prompts, or model comparisons;
- promises, reassurance, or transitions that do not teach anything.

Delete product language, generic encouragement, setup announcements, and sentences that merely restate headings, buttons, visible order, or task rules. Retain concrete instructions, necessary empty-state guidance, observable feedback, safety constraints, and reasoning required for recall.

## Preserve Source Boundaries

- Keep source quotations and transcriptions unchanged and visibly distinguish them from added teaching copy.
- Verify every reviewer suggestion against the source and the actual interaction.
- Do not let a copy review silently change teaching goals, UI, state, algorithms, source material, or assessment rules.
- A copy reviewer may remove redundant copy and containers that become empty. Any broader redesign requires separate learner authorization.

## External Review

Use the reviewer and reasoning mode the learner requested. Report the actual model and mode used; do not silently substitute another one.

When review happens through a browser chat, send only rendered screenshots and learner-visible plain text. Do not upload HTML, CSS, TypeScript, generated JavaScript, repository files, hidden notes, or unrelated source material. Treat reviewer output as suggestions, not authority.

## Final Check

- Every sentence either teaches, directs a necessary action, reports a result, or states a real constraint.
- The lesson contains no chat residue or AI self-reference.
- Source words remain locked.
- Terminology, tone, and reading level are consistent.
- Copy still matches the rendered UI and current interaction.
