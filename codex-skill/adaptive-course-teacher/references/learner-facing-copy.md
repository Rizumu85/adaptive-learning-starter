# Learner-Facing Copy

Use this workflow before publishing AI-written teaching copy. The current agent performs every pass; no second model, paid service, browser chat, or additional skill is required. Source quotations, transcript excerpts, and instructor wording remain locked unless the learner explicitly asks for an editorial transformation.

## Two Passes

Review the whole artifact, not isolated sentences.

1. **Editorial pass:** mark each block `delete`, `retain`, or `shorten` in working notes. Ask whether it teaches something necessary or enables the current action. Delete unnecessary material rather than polishing it into a more pleasant distraction. Check headings, button labels, captions, and nearby instructions for repetition.
2. **Writing pass:** rewrite the retained content as a teacher addressing this learner. Reorder or combine the added explanation when that makes the teaching clearer; do not merely preserve every original sentence and replace its vocabulary. Keep the authorized learning objective and factual meaning intact.

Read the result as a continuous lesson. A locally polished sentence can still repeat the title, adjacent control, or previous paragraph.

## Write Like Teaching Material

Study a short relevant passage from the supplied textbook or course for sentence length, terminology, question style, and reading level. Use those observations to guide new teaching copy without altering quotations, imitating every surface feature, or pretending added text came from the source. A beginner's textbook does not mean an adult learner should be addressed as a small child.

Prefer concrete questions and actions over generic encouragement, product language, elaborate transitions, and explanations of the page itself. Read the text aloud or check whether it would sound natural when spoken by a teacher. Natural writing does not require slang, deliberate mistakes, or a fixed list of forbidden words.

Examples of editorial decisions, not mandatory replacement phrases:

- “Writing slowly is fine; first see how much you remember.” Delete the reassurance when the exercise already makes the action clear. If an instruction is needed, say what to recall or write.
- “Write three times and stop; if it is still unsteady, practise tomorrow.” Keep this out of teaching copy when it only repeats a limit discussed during design. Retain a specific quantity or stopping condition when it is genuinely part of the current assignment.
- A heading says “Choose a verb and write two sentences.” Do not add a paragraph explaining that this section lets the learner choose a verb and write two sentences. Add only the missing content, such as which verbs or what scene to describe.

## Keep Internal Work Internal

Do not transfer conversation residue into the lesson. Keep these in working notes unless the learner needs them to act or avoid a real error:

- rejected alternatives and implementation history;
- explanations of why the page was designed a certain way;
- arbitrary repetition limits or temporary experiments discussed in chat;
- instructions to the AI, reviewer prompts, or model comparisons;
- promises, reassurance, or transitions that do not teach anything.

Delete product language, generic encouragement, setup announcements, and sentences that merely restate headings, buttons, visible order, or task rules. Retain concrete instructions, necessary empty-state guidance, observable feedback, safety constraints, and reasoning required for recall.

## Preserve Source Boundaries

- Keep source quotations and transcriptions unchanged and visibly distinguish them from added teaching copy.
- Verify every reviewer suggestion against the source and the actual interaction.
- Do not let a copy review silently change teaching goals, UI, state, algorithms, source material, or assessment rules.
- A copy reviewer may remove redundant copy and containers that become empty. Any broader redesign requires separate learner authorization.

## Final Check

- Every sentence either teaches, directs a necessary action, reports a result, or states a real constraint.
- The lesson contains no chat residue or AI self-reference.
- Source words remain locked.
- Terminology, tone, and reading level are consistent.
- Copy still matches the rendered UI and current interaction.
- Read the whole rendered lesson once more after editing. Check whether a necessary instruction disappeared with a redundant paragraph, and whether any retained text gives away an answer during recall.
